KG PLAY SCHOOL - ADMISSION EMAIL SETUP

Upload these files/folders to the ROOT of the GitHub repository:

index.html
script.js
package.json
api/admission.js

Keep your existing styles.css, assets folder and vercel.json unchanged.

VERCEL ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES
1. Open your Vercel project.
2. Go to Settings -> Environment Variables.
3. Add:
   EMAIL_USER = kgplayschool24@gmail.com
   EMAIL_APP_PASSWORD = your Google 16-character App Password
   EMAIL_TO = kgplayschool24@gmail.com
4. Apply the variables to Production (and Preview if desired).
5. Redeploy the latest deployment after saving the variables.

IMPORTANT
- Do NOT put EMAIL_APP_PASSWORD in GitHub, index.html or script.js.
- EMAIL_APP_PASSWORD is not your normal Gmail password.
- The Gmail account normally needs 2-Step Verification enabled before an App Password can be created.
